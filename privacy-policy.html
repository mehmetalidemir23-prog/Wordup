Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · App: WordUp Pro (com.alidemir.worduppro)

1. Summary

  • WordUp Pro is a vocabulary learning app. Your progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect any personal data. The app does not send data to us or to any other server.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud backup. The app works offline.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking tools and no in-app purchases.

2. Developer and contact

The app is developed by Ali Demir. For privacy questions: alidemir23x@gmail.com

3. Stored on your device

Your learning progress, review schedule, settings, username, badges and statistics are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us and we cannot see them.

4. Actions you start

ActionWhat happens
Backup file (Settings → Data Backup)Your progress is written to a file; you choose where to save it and who to share it with.
Share / Invite a friendYour phone's share sheet opens; the text and score image go only to the app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp).
Daily reminderIf you allow it, the notification is scheduled locally on your device; no server is used.
Read aloudWords are read by your device's own text-to-speech engine.
Links (Play Store, Instagram, WhatsApp)Tapping opens that app or your browser; what happens there is covered by that service's privacy policy.

The app does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

5. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. Downloads and updates are provided by Google Play and are covered by Google's privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or delete it with Settings → Reset All Data. We hold no data about you.

7. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have rights to access, correct, delete and object regarding your personal data. Since we hold no data about you, you can delete the data on your device yourself from inside the app. For questions, write to alidemir23x@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

WordUp Pro is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect personal data from anyone.

9. Security

Thanks to Android's app sandbox, your data can only be read by WordUp Pro. The security of an exported backup file depends on where you keep it.

10. Changes

If we add a feature that processes data — such as optional cloud backup, Google sign-in or purchases — this policy will be updated before that feature is released and the change will be announced in the app. Questions about this policy: alidemir23x@gmail.com
